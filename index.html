Background

About

Prior authorizations, coverage rules, and forms that don't talk to each other aren't neutral. They're administrative burden, and they cost patients time, money, and dignity while wearing down the clinicians who serve them. My work is about lifting that burden.

At Michigan Medicine, I lead informatics design and product for statewide collaborative quality initiatives. Coverage Checker began as one workflow to help clinicians navigate diabetes coverage and prior authorization, and grew into a suite supporting more than 20 products (published in Annals of Family Medicine, 2025). Along the way I grew our digital portfolio from 6 to nearly 90 tools and built a UX team from 1 to 6, grounded in 7,000+ hours of participatory design with patients, caregivers, and clinicians.

I bring a UX research background from the University of Michigan School of Information, and I'm now earning my MSW at U-M on the path to clinical licensure. Social work gives me language for what I've seen in health tech: moral injury, structural harm, and the power of collective action. I want to bring that lens to civic technology, building public-interest tools that make systems easier to navigate and kinder to the people inside them.
